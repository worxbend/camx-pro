Position and motor state

Disconnected

Position is an open-loop estimate, not measured feedback.

Support the camera before releasing motors. Releasing, resetting or interrupting motion requires a fresh manual reference.

1. Reference and arm

With motors released, align the mechanism to the stated angles. Zero means centered pan and a level camera. The reference command acknowledges this physical alignment; it does not move or find home.

Released last at . This is only a hint: align the mechanism, then confirm its real angles.

2. Move

Pan and tilt arrive together. Hold a direction to jog. Release to decelerate; wait for motion to finish before changing direction.

Motion feel

Lowers the configured speed, acceleration and jerk for new moves. Nothing is saved or re-referenced; 100 % is the configured ceiling.

Presets

    Saving the current position works while armed once the mechanism is referenced and still; flash is written when the motors are released (see “unsaved” above). Entered angles can be saved only while released.

    Calibration and settings

    Release motors before applying settings. Defaults restrict travel to pan ±20°, tilt ±15°. Set commissioned only after fit, cable, direction and loaded motion checks. Settings changes invalidate the reference.
