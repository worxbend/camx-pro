ENGINEERED AROUND YOUR HARDWARE

Small motors.
A supported mechanism.

The pan motor sits inside the stationary base and drives a four-planet carrier. Two output bearings carry the upper assembly independently of the motor shaft.

The tilt motor sits low behind the camera. A concealed 20T/40T belt drives the bearing-supported cradle, keeping the U-frame clean and the original webcam intact.

What is verified?

Local geometry, mechanical interfaces, motion studies, meshes and print-layer checks have corresponding P3 verification reports.

What still needs a bench?

Actual fit, loaded motor torque, cable behavior, PLA heat and creep. Digital checks do not establish these. This is a fit prototype, not a production-qualified print release.
