# Anker PowerConf C200 reference data

Retrieved 2026-10-20 from [Anker Japan's official A3369 product page](https://www.ankerjapan.com/products/a3369): approximately **55 × 51 × 41 mm**, **83 g**, USB Type-C, rated input **5 V / 1 A**. The page does not label the dimension axes or provide an engineering drawing. These are product specifications, not measurements of a unit.

The [official quick-start guide](https://lp.ankerjapan.com/hubfs/aoos/manual/A3369QSG.pdf) identifies the adjustable clip/stand, tripod thread, rear USB-C port, vents, microphones and privacy switch. Keep all operational surfaces accessible. It does not specify the thread engagement depth, folded clip outline, connector boot, cable bend radius or center of gravity.

P3 reserves a **52 W × 55 D × 55 H mm provisional envelope** and uses the published 83 g mass. The width-axis assignment still requires checking; do not interpret the envelope as a dimensional scan. The existing 25 mm plug projection, 4 mm cable diameter and 20 mm cable bend radius remain assumptions. Confirm the intact folded webcam fits the cradle coupon before printing the complete cradle. Do not tighten a tripod screw until its actual safe engagement is measured.

Material: PLA. Nozzle: **0.4 mm** assumed; confirm it. Use the actual nozzle and PLA manufacturer's profile in Bambu Studio. Validate heat exposure with the motors energized; a room-temperature CAD fit cannot establish PLA creep resistance or service life.
